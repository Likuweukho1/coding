Lesson: Containers in Bootstrap

Overview

In Bootstrap, containers are the main layout building blocks. They hold, align, and center page content. There are two main types: .container and .container-fluid.

This container has a fixed width at different screen sizes. Try resizing your browser window - the width will adjust at certain breakpoints.

The .container-fluid class makes the container stretch across the entire width of the screen. Try resizing your browser — this section always fills the width.
